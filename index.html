Charge Sound – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Summary: Charge Sound does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Who we are

Charge Sound is an app for Android and iPhone published by Charge Sound. It plays a sound you choose when you connect or disconnect your phone's charger.

Information the app uses

  • Battery level and charging state. The app reads these from your phone's operating system to show them on screen and to play your sound when a charger is connected or disconnected. This information is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your settings. Your sound choices, switches and theme are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Audio files you import. If you import your own sound, the app copies that file into its private storage on your device. It is never uploaded.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos or any identifiers.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking services.
  • It does not send any data over the internet. The released Android app does not even request internet access.
  • It does not share data with anyone, because it has none to share.

Permissions

On Android, the app shows a persistent, minimised notification while the charging sound is turned on. Android requires this for any app that reacts to events in the background. The app also asks Android to restart it after the phone restarts, and keeps the phone awake only while a sound is playing. On iPhone, charging sounds rely on a Shortcuts automation that you create and control.

Deleting your data

All of the app's data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it completely.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
